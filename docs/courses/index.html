Open course material I have written in statistics, programming, and academic writing. Each one is a full, self-contained website with worked examples, rendered mathematics where it is needed, and code.

Graduate / early PhD level 13 modules · Theory-first

Bayesian Statistics and Inference

A measure-theoretic, decision-theoretic treatment of Bayesian inference

Derives the major algorithms from first principles, with rigorous proofs of correctness and convergence, posterior analysis (propriety, consistency, identifiability), and both Bayesian and frequentist evaluation perspectives.

  • Foundations & conjugate models Exact inference, regression, GLMs
  • MCMC theory Metropolis–Hastings, Gibbs, advanced methods
  • Approximate inference Variational inference, Gaussian processes
  • Theory & criticism Asymptotics, consistency, model checking

Graduate level 12 chapters · Proof-based

Hidden Markov Models (HMMs)

A rigorous, mathematically heavy course on hidden Markov models

Built up from measure-theoretic probability through inference, estimation theory, and modern variants — proofs and derivations rather than code snippets, with proof-based problem sets throughout.

  • Foundations Markov chains, stochastic matrices, spectral theory
  • Inference algorithms Forward–backward, Viterbi, filtering, smoothing
  • Estimation Maximum likelihood, EM / Baum–Welch, identifiability
  • Advanced theory Asymptotics, continuous states, Kalman filter
  • Applications Speech, bioinformatics, finance, epidemiology
