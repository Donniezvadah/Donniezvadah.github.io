              <li>Asymptotic theory, consistency, and model checking</li>

            </p>

              <li>Maximum likelihood, EM / Baum–Welch, and identifiability</li>
              <li>Asymptotics, continuous-state models, and the Kalman filter</li>
              <li>Applications in speech, bioinformatics, finance, and epidemiology</li>
