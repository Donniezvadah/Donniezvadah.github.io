Research, teaching and professional work in statistics and machine learning.

Research

  • 2025 – present Trinity College Dublin

    T-DIET: longitudinal dietary patterns and health outcomes

    PhD project

    Bayesian methods for longitudinal dietary composition data that link dietary patterns to health outcomes. Hidden Markov and mixture models, implemented in R, identify dietary regimes and track them over time. Supervised by Assistant Professor Silvia D'Angelo and funded by the Research Ireland Frontiers for the Future Programme.

  • Mar – Jun 2025 AIMS South Africa and Stellenbosch University

    Large language models and sequential decision-making

    MSc project, Distinction

    The project assessed how well large language models solve sequential decision-making problems. It characterised the models' behaviour on the multi-armed bandit problem against classical bandit algorithms. Supervised by Prof Audrey Durand, Université Laval.

  • Jun – Aug 2025 Sendai, Japan

    Key relay protocol for quantum key distribution

    g-RIPS-Sendai, Team Mitsubishi B

    Industrial research project on key relay protocols for quantum key distribution. The Advanced Institute for Materials Research (AIMR) at Tohoku University runs g-RIPS-Sendai with the Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics (IPAM) at UCLA.
